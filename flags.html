Flags

Flag definitions are specific to your agency. A flag's category determines how it is raised: Timer, Expiration and Event flags are raised automatically by rule, while Manual flags are the only ones staff can apply by hand from a constituent profile. Inactive definitions stay on record but are no longer raised or offered.
