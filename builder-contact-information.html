Set up · Contact Information

How will you reach the applicant?

Tick Displayed for each field you want on the application, and Required for the ones the applicant must fill in.

Anything displayed but not required is optional for the applicant. Anything not displayed is never asked for and never stored.

The panel on the right is the applicant’s view of this section, and it updates as you tick. It is a picture of the form, not the form itself, so nothing in it can be typed into.

Pick fields

Field Description Displayed Required
Telephone Number Always collected Primary number, used for anything that needs a call
Alt. Telephone Number A second number to try
Email Address Always collected and required Sign-in address, and where every notice is sent

This is an authenticated application, so the email address is the applicant’s sign-in and the channel for every notice. A telephone number is always collected too, though you can decide whether the applicant has to give one.

Note: because Email Address is displayed, the application also asks the applicant to re-enter it, so a typo cannot lock them out of their own submission. That second field is not listed above because you do not control it, but you will see it in the preview.
