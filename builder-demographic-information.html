Set up · Demographic Information

Any demographic detail to collect?

All five of these start switched on and none of them start required. If your agency does not report on any of it, use Skip to leave the whole section off.

Anything displayed but not required is optional for the applicant. Anything not displayed is never asked for and never stored.

The panel on the right is the applicant’s view of this section, and it updates as you tick. It is a picture of the form, not the form itself, so nothing in it can be typed into.

The first four are dropdowns, and you choose what goes inside them as well as whether they appear. Open the options under a field to add or remove individual entries.

A dropdown needs at least one option, so the two go together: untick every option and the field switches off, and switch a field back on and its options come back with it.

Demographic questions are sensitive, and an applicant who cannot see why you are asking is more likely to abandon the application. Ask for the fewest of these you can, and leave them optional unless the licence itself has to carry them.
