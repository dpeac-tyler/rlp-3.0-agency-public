Now let’s give your application a title, display name, subtitle, description and URL.

The application title matters because it is saved as the unique name for this application. The display title appears at the top of every page of the application and on the certificate the applicant receives, and it can be changed at any time without affecting the saved application title. You can also give the application a subtitle.

The description should be a brief explanation of the application, so applicants clearly understand what they are about to submit.

Note: The More Info URL is not the URL that will be assigned to this application. It is a link to a website that may have more comprehensive information, FAQs and the like, for applicants who need additional detail about the license or permit they are submitting.
