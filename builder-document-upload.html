Define the documents this application collects

Tell the applicant what to bring, then list the documents the submission asks for.

  • Start with the instructions and information the applicant needs about the documents you want.
  • Then add the documents, up to twenty-five. Each one gets details that help the applicant understand what is being asked for, and help your staff manage what comes back.
  • Later, in Question Builder and Conditions, you can map these documents to questions. Any document you do not map appears in its own Document Upload section on the application instead.

Note: a document used by a condition cannot be edited here. Remove the condition on the Conditions step first, edit the document, then reapply the rule.
