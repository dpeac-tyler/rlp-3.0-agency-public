Let's build your application

Here's the whole path first: eight steps, grouped into four phases. Everything saves as a draft, and nothing goes live until you publish in step 8.

What you'll do in the 8 steps

Read the full guide
  1. 1

    Application Details

    Type, name, internal form number, description and a help URL.

  2. 2

    Applicant Information

    Decide what you need to collect from the applicant or entity.

  3. 3

    Question Builder

    Add the questions you need, choosing from the question types.

  4. 4

    Conditions Optional

    Show or hide questions based on earlier answers.

  5. 5

    Payment

    Set the price of the license or permit.

  6. 6

    Preview

    See the application exactly as your applicants will.

  7. 7

    License

    Effective dates and an optional certificate template.

  8. 8

    Notification

    The message applicants get when the license is issued.

Start with the license and application type

These two choices decide which later steps appear, and you can change them until Title Information is complete. Your agency has 24 license types to choose from, and the application type is what the applicant is doing, whether that's an original application, a renewal, or an update to a license they already hold. Fields marked Required must be completed.
