Commercial Fishing Permit

Required to take and sell fish from Illinois waters commercially. The permit covers one vessel and runs for the calendar year.

Instructions

You will need your vessel's state registration number and the address where the vessel is moored. If you held a commercial permit in the last 3 years, have that permit number ready too.

Your progress is saved as you go. You can leave and come back to finish before you pay.
